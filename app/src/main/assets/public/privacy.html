Privacy Policy — Regional Panchang

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Regional Panchang ("the App", "we", "us") provides Hindu Panchang, festival, and horoscope information for every Indian state and union territory, through calendar.sigq.in and the Regional Panchang app for Android and iOS. This page explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have.

Mobile Apps (Android & iOS): Fully Offline

The Regional Panchang app for Android and iOS computes all Panchang, festival, lunar-month, and Rashifal data on your device using a built-in astronomical engine. The mobile apps work with no internet connection at all.

  • No location data leaves your phone. If you allow location access, the rounded (≈1 km) coordinates are used on-device to suggest your state and calculate sunrise/sunset. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The mobile apps do not contact any server for their core features.
  • Third-party services listed below (Nominatim, Gemini, freehoroscopeapi) apply to the website, calendar.sigq.in, only — not to the offline mobile apps.

Information We Collect (Website)

Location (optional). If you allow it, the website uses your browser's location to automatically suggest which state's Panchang system to show you — for example, Tamil Panchangam versus Gujarati Panchang. This is entirely optional: you can skip it and choose your state manually from the App's own list at any time. Before your coordinates are used for anything, they are rounded to roughly a 1 km area — the App never calculates, transmits, or stores your exact GPS position. The rounded coordinate is used only to (a) calculate sunrise and sunset for the Panchang, and (b) look up which Indian state you're in.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, precise location, health data, payment information, or any other information that identifies you personally. The App has no user accounts, logins, or profiles.

Standard technical logs. Like virtually all web services, our server automatically logs basic technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are not linked to any user profile, because the App doesn't have profiles.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a small number of external services to provide the App's features. None of them receive your name, email, or anything else that identifies you personally.

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — when you use the "detect my region" feature, we send your rounded (≈1 km) coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to resolve them to an Indian state. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API — used to help generate general horoscope text (Rashifal). Only a date and a language are sent, never anything about you individually. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • freehoroscopeapi.com — provides daily/weekly/monthly zodiac horoscope text. Only a zodiac sign (e.g. "Aries") is sent, never anything about you individually.

We do not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers or advertisers, and the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Retention

Panchang and horoscope results are cached on our server so we don't have to recompute them for every request. Cache entries are keyed to a date, an Indian state, and a rounded (≈1 km) coordinate — never to a specific person or device. Because nothing in our systems identifies who made a particular request, we're not able to look up or delete "your" individual entry on request. Uninstalling the App, or clearing your browser's site data for calendar.sigq.in, removes everything stored on your own device. If you have a specific concern, contact us below and we'll do what we reasonably can.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age where you live).

Your Choices

  • Skip or deny the location prompt at any time and choose your state manually instead.
  • Uninstall the App, or clear site data in your browser, to remove anything stored locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever we do.
